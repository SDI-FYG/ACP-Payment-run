What the run does, in this order

  1. Lines with a closedAtDate go to tab Error (red); the column is then dropped.
  2. The Applied filters footer line and the columns YourReference and DuePeriod are removed.
  3. Vendor 99999 Xpenditure and all lines with an Interco value are left out.
  4. Same externalDocumentNumber (signs ignored) and amount within ±1 EUR: reported in Poss Duplicat, kept in Lines in red.
  5. Lines that net to zero per company, vendor and currency, or +/- pairs of the same amount: Is Zero (green), with a total per vendor.
  6. paymentMethod DOM, DOMI, DOMICIL, CCARD: tab paymentMethod.
  7. Lines with an onHold code: tab onHold (yellow); that vendor's other lines turn light yellow in Lines.
  8. Customer lines: tab Cust-Vend (blue) with the same vendor's lines, which stay in Lines in light blue.
  9. Credit lines due after run date + 7 days are dropped; debit lines stay with a yellow dueDate.
  10. Vendors with a debit balance in EUR per company: tab Total Debit, with a total per vendor.
  11. Lines is sorted by vendorNumber and dueDate, then tab Currency pivots it per company and currency.
